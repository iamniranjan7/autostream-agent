# AutoStream — Product Knowledge Base

## About AutoStream

AutoStream is a SaaS product that provides automated video editing tools for content creators. Our AI-powered platform helps YouTubers, Instagram creators, TikTokers, and other video creators save time on editing so they can focus on creating great content.

## Pricing Plans

### Basic Plan — $29/month
- **Videos per month:** 10 videos
- **Maximum resolution:** 720p
- **AI captions:** Not included
- **Support:** Standard email support (business hours)
- **Best for:** Hobbyist creators just getting started with video content

### Pro Plan — $79/month
- **Videos per month:** Unlimited videos
- **Maximum resolution:** 4K
- **AI captions:** Included — automatic caption generation in 50+ languages
- **Support:** 24/7 priority support via chat and email
- **Best for:** Professional creators and businesses who need high-volume, high-quality video output

## Key Features

- **Auto-Cut:** Automatically removes silences, filler words, and dead air from your videos
- **Smart Transitions:** AI-powered scene detection and smooth transition insertion
- **Color Grading:** One-click cinematic color grading presets powered by AI
- **Audio Enhancement:** Noise removal and audio leveling for professional sound quality
- **Multi-Platform Export:** Export in optimized formats for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and more
- **AI Captions (Pro only):** Automatic subtitle generation with multi-language support

## Company Policies

### Refund Policy
- Full refund available within the first **7 days** of purchase
- **No refunds** are issued after the 7-day period
- To request a refund within the eligible window, contact support@autostream.ai

### Support Policy
- **Basic Plan:** Email support during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 6 PM EST)
- **Pro Plan:** 24/7 support available via live chat and email

### Free Trial
- AutoStream currently does **not** offer a free trial
- Users can subscribe to the Basic Plan to explore the platform at an affordable price

### Cancellation
- Users can cancel their subscription at any time from their account dashboard
- After cancellation, access continues until the end of the current billing cycle
